Multiple Entry Points

Language Support

Glossary of Key Terms (English and Chinese where needed):

  • Professional Capital: 專業資本 - The combination of human, social, and decisional capital
  • Accountability: 問責制 - Being answerable for professional decisions and outcomes
  • Autonomy: 自主權 - Professional freedom to make pedagogical decisions
  • Ethics: 道德標準 - Moral principles guiding professional conduct

Sentence Starters for Case Study:

  • "From a [context] perspective, the principal's expectation may be justified because..."
  • "However, considering [another context], Ms. Lam's concern about..."
  • "A balanced approach would involve..."

🎓 Teaching Notes & Tips

Success Indicators (Social Constructivism)

  • High engagement in pair/group talk (>80% of students contribute)
  • Jamboard shows evidence of multiple perspectives and negotiated meaning (not just cut-and-paste)
  • Post-test shows increase in nuanced understanding
  • Students reference peers' ideas in their reflections

Modification Strategies

  • If groups dominated by few voices: Introduce rotating roles (facilitator, recorder, reporter) in next lesson
  • If case study discussion shallow: Add a short video interview with a teacher in a similar dilemma
  • For larger classes: Use online breakout rooms more systematically with timed tasks
  • If time runs short: Move gallery walk to asynchronous (view Jamboards online, post sticky notes digitally)
