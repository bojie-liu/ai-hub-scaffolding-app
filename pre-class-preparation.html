Pre-Class Preparation (Flipped Component)

Since Social Constructivism benefits from shared prior knowledge, please complete the following before class:

📚 Required Readings

Reading 1: Professional Capital

Source: Professional Capital: Transforming Teaching in Every School by Hargreaves & Fullan (2012) – Chapter 1

Focus: Definitions of professional capital

Access: Available via university library or online access

Reading 2: Comparative Perspective

Source: "Teacher Professionalism in Finland and the United States"

Focus: Comparative analysis of professionalism frameworks

Access: University library link or open access journal

🎥 Pre-Class Video (5 minutes)

Watch: What is Teacher Professionalism? A Global Snapshot

This short video provides an overview of teacher professionalism from international perspectives.

Options:

  • Instructor-made video (available on LMS)
  • OECD video on teaching status (YouTube)

Make notes on key concepts while watching.

💭 Guiding Questions

Please reflect on these questions and post your responses on the discussion board before class:

  1. What do you think are the three most important elements of a "professional" teacher?
  2. From the readings, find one similarity and one difference between how professionalism is described in two different countries.
  3. Bring one question that puzzled you about the readings.

🌐 How to Post on Discussion Board

  1. Log into your Learning Management System (LMS)
  2. Navigate to the course page
  3. Click on "Discussions" in the sidebar
  4. Find the thread: "Pre-Class Reflections: Teacher Professionalism"
  5. Click "Reply" and type your responses to the three questions
  6. Submit your post at least 24 hours before class
