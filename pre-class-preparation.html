🧪 Diagnostic Quiz

Complete this quiz to check your current understanding. Your teacher will review common mistakes in class.

Question 1

In the number 582, what does the digit 8 represent?

(a) 8 ones
(b) 8 tens
(c) 8 hundreds

Question 2

How many tens are in 240?

(a) 2
(b) 4
(c) 24

Question 3

Which is bigger: 89 or 91?

(a) 89
(b) 91

Question 4

Answer: 600 + 30 + 5

💡 Guiding Question

Bring to class one example of a number larger than 100 that you see in your daily life (e.g., page numbers, prices, sports scores). Be ready to share!

📊 Common Misconceptions to Address

  • Confusing "tens in a number" with "digit in tens place" (Q2)
  • Comparing ones before tens when ordering numbers (Q3)
  • Difficulty representing numbers with zeros (prepare reteach for 402, 308)
