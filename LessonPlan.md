# Updated Lesson Plan: Teacher Professionalism in Hong Kong (150 minutes)
**Course**: Teacher Professionalism
**Class Size**: 35 students

---

## **1. Intended Learning Outcomes (ILOs)**
By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
1. **Analyze** key components of teacher professionalism through local (Hong Kong) and international frameworks (e.g., NCATE, INTO), referencing academic journals and policy documents.
2. **Evaluate** ethical dilemmas using the Hong Kong Teachers' Professional Conduct Guidelines (TPCG) as a reference, applying scholarly definitions of professionalism.
3. **Apply** understanding of professional standards to real-world teaching scenarios and connect these to their first teaching practicum experiences.

---

## **2. Pre-Class Preparation**
- **Video**: [Watch: TED-Ed "What Makes a Good Teacher?" (8 mins)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7d8NX5iZUMM)
- **Reading**: [Official EDB Guidelines on Teachers' Professional Conduct (2021)](https://www.edb.gov.hk/en/teacher/guidelines_tpc/index.html) (Sections 1–3, 15 mins) & [Excerpt from "Teacher Professionalism: International Policies and Teacher Education Practices" (5 mins)](https://www.ibe.unesco.org/en/sites/default/files/media-links/teacher-professionalism-policy-paper.pdf).
- **Pre-Test**: 5-question quiz on [Mentimeter](https://mentimeter.com) (e.g., "What is the core duty of a teacher's role according to TPCG?").
- **Guiding Question**: *"How did your teaching practicum experience relate to the concept of teacher professionalism?"*

---

## **3. Teaching & Learning Activities**

### **Introduction (20 minutes)**
- **Hook (10 mins)**:
  Show a 3-minute Hong Kong Education Bureau video clip of a teacher-student interaction scenario (e.g., handling a disruptive student). Ask: *"How would you evaluate the teacher's professionalism here?"* Facilitate whole-class discussion, linking responses to their practicum experiences.
- **Pre-Test Review (5 mins)**:
  Share anonymized pre-test results. Highlight misconceptions (e.g., conflating "friendliness" with professionalism).
- **Brainstorming Questions (5 mins)**:
  Use [Padlet Activity] with prompts like:
  1. *"In one word, how would you describe the most important aspect of teacher professionalism?"*
  2. *"What are three common ethical challenges teachers face in Hong Kong schools?"*
  3. *"Can you recall a time when you saw a teacher violate or exemplify professionalism during your practicum?"*

---

### **Development Activities (115 minutes)**

#### **Part 1: Defining Teacher Professionalism (30 mins)**
- **Interactive Lecture + Think-Pair-Share (20 mins)**:
  Define professionalism using a graphic organizer with citations (e.g., Linda Darling-Hammond 2017, EDB 2021). Pause at 10 mins:
  *"How would TPCG respond to a teacher who leaks exam papers—ethically and procedurally?"*
- **Animated Illustration (10 mins)**:
  Show animated Venn diagram comparing international standards (NCATE vs. TPCG). Pop-up question: *"Which standard emphasizes cultural sensitivity most?"*
- **Pop-Up Quiz (5 mins)**: [Quiz: Teacher Professionalism Components (5 questions)]

**Break (15 minutes)**

#### **Part 2: Case Study & Ethics (60 mins)**
- **Case Study Activity (30 mins)**:
  Distribute *EDB Case Report: Teacher Dismissed for Inappropriate Online Behavior* (2019). Groups discuss:
  *"How did the teacher violate TPCG? What safeguards could prevent this?"*
  Share conclusions via Padlet.
- **Video Sharing (15 mins)**:
  Watch [EDB documentary clip: "Ethical Challenges in Practice" (10 mins)](https://www.edb.gov.hk/video). Q&A: *"What TPCG guidelines were upheld?"*
- **Brainstorming Session (15 mins)**:
  [Padlet Activity] Pose questions like *"How might TPCG's cultural sensitivity clause apply in schools with ethnic minorities?"*

#### **Part 3: Professional Ethics Application (20 mins)**
- **Role-Play Scenarios (15 mins)**:
  Group scenarios: (1) Accepting a parent's gift; (2) Addressing student-to-student discrimination. Peer-assess using TPCG rubric.
- **Pop-Up Poll (5 mins)**: [Mentimeter: "Which TPCG guideline is hardest to uphold in practice?"]

---

### **Synthesis & Closure (15 minutes)**
- **Post-Test (5 mins)**:
  Reuse 2 pre-test questions (e.g., *"What is TPCG's duty of care timeframe?"*). Compare with pre-test results.
- **Reflective Discussion (5 mins)**:
  *"How did today's discussion align with or challenge your practicum experiences?"*
- **Preview Next Session (5 mins)**:
  Legal obligations under Hong Kong's Education Ordinance.

---

## **4. Assessment Methods**

### **Formative Assessment**
- **Pre-Post Test**: Track knowledge gains (e.g., 60% → 90% mastery).
- **Padlet/Role-Play Analysis**: Observe critical thinking in applying guidelines.
- **Exit Ticket**: Collect reflections to assess synthesis.

### **Summative Assessment**
- **Individual Case Study Analysis (Due Week 2)**:
  Analyze an EDB-published misconduct case using the [TPCG Rubric: Ethics Application (4 criteria)](https://www.edb.gov.hk).

## **7. Differentiation & Inclusivity**
- **Practicum Relevance**: Use scenarios based on common student reports (e.g., classroom management challenges).
- **Language Support**: Provide EDB materials in Chinese and English.
- **Advanced Extension**: Challenge groups to compare EDB guidelines with UNESCO's global standards.

---

## **8. Reflection & Improvement**
- **Success Metrics**: High Padlet activity (e.g., 80% participation), 20% score improvement post-test.
- **Feedback**: Use anonymized post-lesson surveys (e.g., Google Forms).
- **Improvements**: Adjust role-play scenarios based on exit tickets.
