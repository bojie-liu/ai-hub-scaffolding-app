# University Lesson Plan: Teacher Professionalism

## 1. Intended Learning Outcomes (ILOs)
- **ILO 1**: Synthesize the concept of teacher professionalism through comparative analysis of global and local definitions [Compare - Blooms Taxonomy].
- **ILO 2**: Evaluate real-world Hong Kong teaching scenarios using ethical guidelines from EDB documentation [Apply - Blooms Taxonomy].
- **ILO 3**: Critically analyze the evolving nature of teacher professionalism in response to societal changes [Analyze - Blooms Taxonomy].

## 2. Pre-Class Preparation
- **Reading Material**: [EDB Hong Kong Guidelines on Professional Conduct](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/teacher/guidelines_tpc/guidelines_en.pdf) (Section 1 and 2 only).
- **Pre-Test**: [Quiz: Teacher Professionalism - Historical vs Modern Perspectives & Ethical Dilemmas].
- **Guiding Questions**:
  1. What societal factors shape teacher expectations in Hong Kong vs. Finland?
  2. How might the EDB guidelines help address cyberbullying cases like the 2022 Tuen Mun School incident?

## 3. Teaching & Learning Activities

### Introduction (10 min)
- **Hook**: Project 2022 Hong Kong news headline: "Teacher Suspended for Social Media Conflicts - Professional Boundary Violation?"
- **Pre-Test Review**: Mentimeter word cloud of "professional teacher traits" with top responses discussed orally.
- **Real-World Connection**: Short video clip of Hong Kong teacher award ceremony highlights.

### Development Activities (65 min)

#### Conceptual Framing (15 min)
- **Interactive Lecture**:
  - Timeline of professionalism evolution (medieval guild to 21st-century HK guidelines).
  - **Think-Pair-Share**: Debate: Should teacher professionalism include personal conduct outside school hours?

#### Comparative Analysis (20 min)
- **Jigsaw Activity**:
  - Groups compare HK EDB guidelines with 1 other system (e.g., Finland's self-driven model, Ontario's OCT standards).
  - **Guiding Questions**:
    1. How do collective cultural values in HK shape the mandatory reporting requirements in Section 3.4?
    2. What conflicts might arise for HK teachers in social media spaces outlined in Guideline 2.7?

#### Ethical Applications (25 min)
- **Case Study Analysis**:
  - **Case 1 (2018 Yuen Long School Leak)**: Student exam paper photos appeared online—was the teacher's disciplinary action justified per Guideline 2.2?
  - **Case 2 (Cyberbullying Scenario)**: Parent complaint about a teacher's critical social media post toward a student's dress code violation.
  - **Role-Play**: Designate group roles (Principal, Affected Teacher, Parent Rep, EDB Investigator) to practice guideline-based resolutions.
- **Mentimeter Live Poll**: "Should teachers lose credentials for personal social media posts?"

#### Closure (5 min)
- Post-test comparison with pre-test data
- "One-minute paper" exit ticket: "How might the EDB guidelines need updating for emerging tech challenges?"
- Preview of next lesson: "Evaluating teacher unions' roles in professionalism standards evolution"

## 4. Assessment Methods

### Formative
- Pre/post test score comparison (multiple choice knowledge growth)
- Padlet participation in case study discussions
- Observation checklist for group dynamics

### Summative
- Individual Written Analysis (due next week):
  - Analyze *two* recent Hong Kong education headlines using EDB guidelines.
  - **Rubric**:
    - Contextual awareness (ILO 1 - 30%)
    - Ethical application (ILO 2 - 40%)
    - Policy critique (ILO 3 - 30%)

## 5. Constructive Alignment Matrix

| Learning Outcome | Teaching Activity | Assessment Method          |
|------------------|------------------|----------------------------|
| Synthesize concept (ILO 1) | Comparative jigsaw, interactive lecture | Summative written analysis (contextual awareness) |
| Evaluate scenarios (ILO 2) | Case study role-play, live polls | Padlet contributions, final paper ethical application |
| Analyze evolution (ILO 3) | Current case studies, exit ticket | Final paper policy critique |

## 8. Reflection & Improvement
- **Success Indicators**: 80% pre/post test score improvement on ethics questions
- **Feedback Mechanism**: Google Form 3-2-1 reflection (3 takeaways, 2 questions, 1 suggestion)
- **Future Modifications**: Partner with HKU/EDB panelists for live Q&A if engagement remains high with real case studies

---

**Hong Kong Context Integration**
Throughout the lesson, specific emphasis was placed on Hong Kong's unique landscape:
- Cultural juxtaposition of traditional teacher authority vs. globalization demands
- Legal framework in Education Ordinance referenced in all scenarios
- Real-world EDB enforcement examples to ground theoretical knowledge
