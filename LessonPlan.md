**University Lesson Plan: Professional Ethics in Teaching Practice**
**Course**: Teacher Professionalism (Test 03) | **Duration**: 150 minutes | **Class Size**: 40 students | **Target**: Year 4 undergraduates
**Language**: English | **Context**: Hong Kong education system

---

### **1. Intended Learning Outcomes (ILOs)**
By the end of the lesson, students will be able to:
1. **Analyze** ethical dilemmas in Hong Kong teaching contexts using EDB guidelines. *(Bloom: Analyze)*
2. **Evaluate** attributes of a professional teacher through self-assessment and peer feedback. *(Bloom: Evaluate)*
3. **Apply** principles of whole person development to address student diversity in schools. *(Bloom: Apply)*
4. **Critique** education equity policies in Hong Kong using case studies. *(Bloom: Evaluate)*

---

### **2. Pre-Class Preparation**
**Materials**:
- Reading: [Guidelines on Professional Conduct for Teachers](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/teacher/guidelines_tpc/guidelines_en.pdf) (Focus on Sections 2.1–2.3, 3.1).
- Video: [YouTube: Ethical Challenges in Hong Kong Classrooms](https://example.com).

**Pre-Test (Online Quiz)**:
1. What are the four key domains of teacher professionalism in Hong Kong?
2. Define "whole person development" in education.
3. True/False: Teacher accountability includes reporting unethical practices of colleagues.

**Guiding Questions**:
- Recall an ethical issue you observed during teaching practice. How did you resolve it?
- How might "education equity" differ in rural vs. urban Hong Kong schools?

---

### **3. Teaching & Learning Activities**

#### **Introduction (15 minutes)**
**Hook**:
- Show a short video clip of a Hong Kong teacher whistleblowing on unfair grading policies.
- Provocative question: *"Is it ethical to withhold grades to encourage improvement? Why or why not?"*

**Pre-Test Discussion**:
- Review quiz results using Mentimeter polls to highlight common misconceptions (e.g., conflating equality with equity).
- Share anonymized survey quotes from former students on challenges of upholding EDB guidelines.

---

#### **Development (105 minutes)**

**Activity 1: Ethical Dilemma Case Study Analysis (30 minutes)**
- **Task**: Analyze [Case study: A teacher discovers a parent falsifying their child's Special Educational Needs (SEN) documentation to access exam accommodations](https://example.com/case1).
- **Process**:
  1. Small groups (4–5 students) discuss using a decision-making framework (e.g., "Identify Stakeholders → Legal/Ethical Implications → EDB Guidelines").
  2. Groups present solutions; peers critique using the hashtag **#EdEthicsHK**.
- **Brainteaser**: "Should teachers prioritize student well-being over school policies in such cases?"

**Activity 2: Role-Play – Attributes of a Professional Teacher (20 minutes)**
- **Task**: Role-play scenarios (e.g., handling a parent complaint, addressing plagiarism).
- **Process**:
  1. Students self-assign roles (teacher, student, parent, principal).
  2. Reflect on [EDB Guideline 3.1](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/teacher/guidelines_tpc/guidelines_en.pdf) during a debrief.
- **Brainstorming Question**: "What traits define a 'role model' teacher in Hong Kong's multicultural schools?"

**Activity 3: Whole Person Development in Practice (25 minutes)**
- **Task**: Critique a mock school policy (e.g., mandatory uniform rules vs. student self-expression).
- **Process**:
  1. Groups redesign the policy to align with [EDB's Life-Wide Learning Framework](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/pshe/edb-guidelines.pdf).
  2. Present using a Padlet board with visual aids.
- **Brainteaser**: "How can career guidance programs for DSE students promote whole person development?"

**Activity 4: Equity vs. Equality Debate (30 minutes)**
- **Task**: Analyze Hong Kong's school funding disparities (e.g., International vs. Local schools).
- **Process**:
  1. Divide class into proponents of "funding equality" vs. "funding equity."
  2. Use a "Yes, But..." debate format to refine arguments.
- **Brainteaser**: "Is providing free textbooks to all students equitable, or does it exacerbate inequality?"

---

#### **Synthesis & Closure (20 minutes)**
**Post-Test**:
- Kahoot quiz with 5 questions (e.g., "What does EDB Guideline 2.3 emphasize?") to measure learning gain.

**Reflection**:
- Exit ticket:
  1. "What is one ethical principle you will prioritize in your first year as a teacher?"
  2. "How did your teaching practice experiences shape your understanding of professionalism?"

---

### **4. Assessment Methods**

**Formative**:
- Observation of group discussions and role-play.
- Exit ticket responses and post-test results.

**Summative**:
- **Case Study Analysis (Individual Assignment)**:
  - Rubric: *Analysis depth (30%), EDB guideline citation (25%), Practical solutions (30%), Clarity (15%)*.

---

### **5. Constructive Alignment Matrix**

| Learning Outcome | Teaching Activity | Assessment Method |
|------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| ILO 1 | Case study analysis | Case study assignment |
| ILO 2 | Role-play and peer feedback | Exit ticket self-reflection |
| ILO 3 | Policy redesign task | Padlet presentation |
| ILO 4 | Equity debate | Debates and post-test |

---

### **6. Required Resources & Technology**
- LMS (e.g., Moodle) for pre-class materials.
- Mentimeter for polls; Padlet for group work.
- Printed role-play scenarios and EDB guideline excerpts.
- Projector for video clips and case study display.

---

### **7. Differentiation & Inclusivity**
- **Support**: Provide multilingual glossaries (Cantonese/English) for policy terms.
- **Extensions**: Advanced students compare HK vs. international equity policies.
- **Accommodations**: Offer alternative formats (e.g., audio summaries for visually impaired learners).

---

### **8. Reflection & Improvement**
- **Success Indicators**: 75% of students score 80%+ on post-test; active participation in debates.
- **Feedback Tools**: Anonymous Google Form for lesson feedback.
- **Future Enhancements**: Include guest lectures from EDB representatives or practicing teachers.

---
**Contextual Integration**: All activities incorporate Hong Kong-specific policies, cases, and terminology. The lesson emphasizes new teachers' challenges (e.g., balancing institutional rules vs. ethical obligations).
