# **Hong Kong Environment Science: Pollution Control and Waste Management Lesson Plan**

---

## **1. Intended Learning Outcomes (ILOs)**
By the end of the session, students will be able to:
1. **Compare** pollution control strategies in Hong Kong with global best practices (Bloom's: Analyze).
2. **Evaluate** the effectiveness of waste management policies in reducing landfill dependency (Bloom's: Evaluate).
3. **Design** a localized solution for household waste reduction using circular economy principles (Bloom's: Create).
4. **Analyze** case studies of pollution crises in Hong Kong (e.g., microplastics in Victoria Harbour) to propose mitigation plans (Bloom's: Analyze).

---

## **2. Pre-Class Preparation**
### **Flipped Learning Component**
- **Pre-Video**: Watch the 10-minute [Hong Kong Environmental Protection Department Video on Waste Management](https://www.environment.gov.hk).
- **Pre-Test**: Complete a 5-question Mentimeter quiz on basic pollution terms (e.g., "Define 'landfill leachate'").
- **Guiding Questions**:
  - What are the three biggest waste management challenges in Hong Kong?
  - How does a waste hierarchy pyramid work, and why is it relevant here?

---

## **3. Teaching & Learning Activities**
### **Introduction (25 minutes)**
- **Hook Activity**: Display a timelapse video of Hong Kong's landfills reaching capacity. Ask: "How will Hong Kong manage waste when all landfills close by 2035?"
- **Pre-Test Review**: Use live polling on Mentimeter to discuss common misconceptions (e.g., "Recycling alone solves waste issues").
- **Real-World Connection**: Link to recent news: HK's 2023 mandatory food waste recycling trial in restaurants.

### **Development Activities (125 minutes)**
#### **Interactive Lecture Segment 1: Pollution Control in HK (20 mins)**
- Discuss air, water, and land pollution sources using [Hong Kong's Environment Bureau Infographic](https://www.epic.gov.hk).
- **Think-Pair-Share**: "Why is vehicle emissions regulation harder in HK than in Berlin?"

#### **Animated Visualization (15 mins)**
- Show animation of Hong Kong's **Waste Hierarchy Pyramid** (refuse, reduce, reuse, recycle, recover, dispose).
- Pause at each level to ask: "Is HK succeeding at this level? Why/why not?"

#### **Small Group Work: Case Study Analysis (30 mins)**
- Analyze the 2017 Hong Kong Plastic Crisis (8 million plastic particles per km² in bay).
- Groups (6 students) answer: "How would you mitigate microplastic pollution using pollution control frameworks?" Provide a case study handout with key data.

#### **Peer Teaching (25 mins)**
- Assign each group to present solutions for one waste stream (plastic/textiles/food). Use Padlet to share slides.

#### **Formative Quiz (15 mins)**
- Kahoot quiz with 10 questions (e.g., "Which HK policy targets 'reduce' in the waste hierarchy?").

#### **Break (10 mins)**

#### **Case Study Workshop (20 mins)**
- Analyze a **marine pollution event** (e.g., 2018 sunken vessel oil spill in Mirs Bay). Students propose a mitigation plan using the [Marine Department's contingency framework].

---

### **Synthesis & Closure (30 minutes)**
- **Post-Test**: 5-question quiz on new concepts (e.g., "Explain circular economy principles in household waste").
- **Reflective Discussion**: "How might Hong Kong adapt Sweden's 'zero-waste society' model?"
- **Preview**: Introduce next session's topic: "Air Pollution and Policy Advocacy."

---

## **4. Assessment Methods**
### **Formative**
- Mentimeter pre/post-test comparison.
- Group case study reports (2 pages max) on marine pollution, due within 48 hours.

### **Summative**
- **Individual Assignment**: Submit a 1,000-word "Waste Reduction Proposal for Hong Kong" using ILOs 1-4.
- **Rubric**:
  - Analysis of HK's current policies (30%)
  - Proposed solution creativity (30%)
  - Case study application (40%)

## **7. Differentiation & Inclusivity**
- **Visual Learners**: Animated pyramid and timelapse video.
- **Kinesthetic Learners**: Padlet slide creation.
- **Advanced Learners**: Optional extension: Compare HK with Tokyo's waste policies.
- **Accessibility**: Subtitles for videos, colorblind-friendly fonts.

---

## **8. Reflection & Improvement**
- **Feedback**: Exit ticket asking, "What concept was most challenging? Why?"
- **Indicators**: 80% pass rate on Kahoot quiz, 90% group participation.
- **Iteration**: For future classes, integrate a guest speaker from HK's Environmental Bureau.
