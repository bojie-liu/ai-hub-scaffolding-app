**Lesson Plan: Knowledge Management and School Development**
**Duration**: 180 minutes | **Class Size**: 90 students | **Student Level**: First-year undergraduates

---

### **1. Intended Learning Outcomes (ILOs)**
By the end of this session, students will be able to:
1. **Analyze** how rapid IT and internet development impacts knowledge creation and sharing in educational settings.
2. **Evaluate** the effectiveness of existing knowledge management (KM) strategies in addressing school leadership challenges.
3. **Create** a tailored KM strategy for a hypothetical school context, integrating knowledge auditing and sharing mechanisms.
4. **Apply** techniques to identify and assess knowledge assets within an organization.

---

### **2. Pre-Class Preparation (Flipped Learning Component)**
- **Pre-Reading**:
  - *Nonaka & Takeuchi's SECI Model* (summary handout) [Existing Material].
  - Case Study: *School A's KM System Failure* (PDF uploaded to LMS).
- **Pre-Test/Diagnostic Quiz**:
  [Quiz: *Key KM Concepts* (5 questions via Moodle quiz)].
  - Example question: *"What is 'tacit knowledge'? Provide an example in a school context."*
- **Guiding Questions**:
  1. "How might cloud storage tools (e.g., Google Workspace) reshape knowledge sharing among teachers?"
  2. "What barriers do school leaders face in preserving institutional knowledge?"

---

### **3. Teaching & Learning Activities**

#### **Introduction (25 minutes)**
- **Hook**: Show a 3-minute video (*"Digital Overload: A School's KM Crisis"*) prompting discussion on IT's mixed impact.
- **Pre-Test Review**: Analyze quiz results in real time using **Mentimeter** word cloud for common misconceptions.
- **Real-World Connection**: Ask, *"How has your prior workplace/school managed knowledge? Was it effective?"*

#### **Development Activities (125 minutes)**

**A. Interactive Lecture: KM Frameworks & IT Disruption (30 minutes)**
- Segment 1: Use **animated SECI model visualization** to illustrate knowledge conversion processes.
- Segment 2: Discuss IT's dual role (e.g., Slack for collaboration vs. fragmented information).
- Think-Pair-Share: *"Describe a KM tool you use. Does it enhance or hinder learning?"*

**B. Small Group Discussion: Case Study Analysis (40 minutes)**
- Groups of 4-5 analyze *School A's KM Failure* (provided).
- Structured prompts:
  1. Identify 2 strengths and 2 weaknesses in the school's KM strategy.
  2. Suggest 1 IT solution to address the weaknesses.
- Deliverable: Groups post summaries on **Padlet** board.

**C. Knowledge Audit Simulation (30 minutes)**
- Provide template for auditing knowledge assets (e.g., staff expertise, digital archives).
- Activity: Simulate audit for a hypothetical underperforming school using fictional data.
- Digital Flashcards: Terms like "knowledge mapping," "communities of practice" via **Kahoot!**

**D. Peer Teaching: KM Strategy Creation (25 minutes)**
- Groups present audit findings and co-construct a KM strategy.
- Use "Speed Dating" format: Rotate groups to refine strategies with feedback from peers.

---

#### **Synthesis & Closure (30 minutes)**
- **Post-Test**: Short online quiz (3 questions mirroring pre-test to measure progress).
- **Reflective Discussion**: Exit ticket via **Mentimeter**: *"One thing I'll apply to improve knowledge sharing at my school."*
- **Preview**: Announce next session on *Implementing KM Systems* (include readings on change management).

---

### **4. Assessment Methods**

#### **Formative**
- **Mentimeter Word Cloud**: Track pre/post-test misconceptions.
- **Padlet Postings**: Quality of case study analysis and audit conclusions.
- **Peer Feedback**: Evaluate participation in speed-dating activity.

#### **Summative**
- **Case Study Assignment** (due next week): Develop a KM plan for a real school context.
  - **Rubric**:
    - Analysis depth (30%)
    - Strategy feasibility (30%)
    - Use of KM frameworks (30%)
    - Creativity/presentation (10%)

---

### **5. Constructive Alignment Matrix**

| Create KM strategy                     | Peer teaching strategy creation       | Case study assignment rubric          |
| Apply knowledge audit techniques       | Audit simulation + flashcards         | Audit deliverable + summative quiz    |

---

### **6. Required Resources & Technology**
- **LMS**: Moodle for pre/post-tests and readings.
- **Tools**: Mentimeter (polls/q&a), Padlet (group boards), Kahoot! (flashcards).
- **Physical**: SECI model poster (printed for classroom display).

---

### **7. Differentiation & Inclusivity**
- **Support**: Provide captioned videos, readable screen materials, and extended time for neurodivergent learners.
- **Advanced Learners**: Optional reading on social network analysis for KM.
- **Multilingual**: Glossary of terms in Traditional Chinese and English.

---

### **8. Reflection & Improvement**
- **Success Indicators**: High quiz score improvement and Padlet activity rates.
- **Feedback**: Anonymized student survey on perceived KM skill gains.
- **Future Modifications**: Replace case study annually with diverse global school examples.

---

**Additional Notes**:
- **Breaks**: 10-minute break after 60 minutes and 15-minute break after 100 minutes.
- **Scalability**: Use Padlet breakout rooms for synchronous group collaboration.
