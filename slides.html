Scaffolding Reading Comprehension

Think-Aloud Modelling + Guided Group Annotation

Course: BEd (English Language)

Duration: 120 minutes

Students: 90 undergraduates (Year 3/4)

Pedagogy: Vygotsky's Zone of Proximal Development

Why ZPD?

"What a child can do with assistance today, she will be able to do by herself tomorrow."
— Lev Vygotsky
  • Learning happens between what learners can do alone and with help
  • Teacher = More Knowledgeable Other (MKO)
  • Peers = social scaffold for reading comprehension
  • Structure: I do → We do → You do

Intended Learning Outcomes

By the end of this session, students will be able to:

  1. Apply a think-aloud protocol to make reading strategies visible
  2. Use collaborative annotation to co-construct meaning
  3. Analyze how ZPD scaffolding moves learners forward
  4. Evaluate scaffolded reading strategies for diverse learners
  5. Design a mini-lesson segment with think-aloud and group annotation

Pre-Class Preparation

10 questions on:

  • Zone of Proximal Development

  • Scaffolding vs. simplifying
  • Fading support & gradual release

Adaptive feedback provided after each answer

Part A — Think-Aloud Modelling

Teacher reads Text A aloud and verbalizes strategies:

  • 📍 Predicting: "I think this will be about..."
  • 🔍 Clarifying: "I'm not sure what this means, let me..."
  • ❓ Questioning: "Why would the author say...?"
  • 🔗 Making connections: "This reminds me of..."
  • 💡 Inferring: "Reading between the lines, I think..."

Then pairs debrief: "What did the teacher do?"

Introduce Annotation Code Card: ?, *, !, C, P, INF

Part B — Guided Group Annotation

15–18 mixed-ability groups of 5–6 students

Each member annotates Text B individually, then shares in group

Roles:

  • Leader — keeps time, ensures everyone speaks
  • Scribe — records annotations on Padlet
  • Clarifier — checks understanding with questions
  • Connector — links to course readings / practice
  • Reporter — shares group thinking in synthesis

Post on Padlet Gallery: one puzzling phrase + one connection + one new understanding

Differentiation and Inclusivity

  • 🔀 Mixed-ability grouping for peer scaffolding
  • 📚 Choice of text difficulty (3–4 versions of Text B)
  • 💬 Role cards with sentence starters for participation support
  • 📹 Captioned videos + accessible digital text
  • 🌐 L1 clarification permitted for complex vocabulary
  • 🎯 Extension tasks: advanced groups create think-aloud scripts

Universal Design for Learning (UDL) embedded throughout

Reflection and Next Steps

Use exit tickets and Padlet to:

  • Identify persistent misconceptions
  • Gauge confidence with strategies
  • Spot groups needing more support

If needed, adjust:

  • Repeat modelling on shorter text
  • Add micro-teaching challenge
  • Rotate roles mid-task

Next Session Goal

Students micro-teach a 2-minute think-aloud to peers — making reading strategies visible, social, and transferable
