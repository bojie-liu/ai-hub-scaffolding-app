Introduction (8 minutes)

Introduction Activity

Duration: 8 minutes (13% of lesson time)

🎯 Hook Activity

The Magic Number Challenge

Display on board: 9,999

Ask students: "What would happen if I added 1 more? What would the number become?"

⏱️ Allow 30 seconds for think time

Expected answer: 10,000

Teaching point: Introduce the 5-digit number boundary. Explain how reaching 10,000 shows the power of place value – each position represents 10 times the value of the position to its right.

📊 Pre-Test Review

Quickly go through the pre-test answers, focusing on common misconceptions.

Common Misconception: "The 5 in 3,521 is just 5"

✓ Correction: The digit 5 is in the hundreds place, so its value is 500, not 5.

Watch for: Students confusing the digit (5) with its value (500)

✓ Strategy: Use consistent language – "The digit 5 is in the hundreds place. Its value is 500."

🌍 Real-World Connection

The $9,999.99 Receipt

Show a sample receipt or image with total: $9,999.99

Discussion: "How does each digit's place determine its value in this price?"

  • The 9 in the thousands place = $9,000
  • The 9 in the hundreds place = $900
  • The 9 in the tens place = $90
  • The 9 in the ones place = $9

👨‍🏫 Teacher Tips

  • Keep the pace brisk – this section is only 8 minutes
  • Use visual aids (number cards, slides) to maintain engagement
  • Invite 2-3 students to share their pre-test thinking
