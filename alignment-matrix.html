🎯 Alignment Principles

This lesson demonstrates constructive alignment through:

  • Vertical alignment: Activities progress from concrete → pictorial → abstract
  • Horizontal alignment: Each ILO has matched teaching and assessment
  • Scaffolded progression: I Do → We Do → You Do throughout
  • Multiple representations: Physical, pictorial, symbolic, and verbal
  • Authentic application: Real-world bundling contexts mirror learning objectives

🌟 What This Means for You

Everything in this lesson is designed to help you learn step by step. You won't be tested on anything you haven't practiced!

  • You learn with blocks (touching and building)
  • You practice with drawings (seeing and drawing)
  • You solve with numbers (thinking and comparing)
  • You apply to real life (using what you learned)
