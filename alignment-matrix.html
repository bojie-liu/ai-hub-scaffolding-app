Biggs' Constructive Alignment Principles

This lesson embeds Biggs' constructive alignment model by ensuring all three elements are coherent:

🎯

ILOs

Outcomes framed using active Bloom's verbs: Apply, Use, Analyze, Evaluate, Design

🧩

Teaching Activities

Gradual-release tasks require students to perform at the cognitive level named in each ILO

📊

Assessment Tasks

Formative checks and the summative mini-lesson directly mirror the ILO verbs
