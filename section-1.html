Section 1 — Intended Learning Outcomes

PSY101 · Introduction to Learning Theories · 7 ILOs across Bloom's Taxonomy
🤖 AI Literacy is an Explicit Learning Outcome

ILOs #6 and #7 ensure students develop critical AI literacy — evaluating accuracy, bias, and limitations of AI outputs, and reflecting on ethical use of AI in psychology coursework. These are not optional extensions; they are core assessable outcomes.

🎯Learning Outcomes Overview
#Intended Learning OutcomeBloom's LevelAlignment
ILO 1 Analyze the key principles of classical conditioning (acquisition, extinction, generalization, discrimination) using real-world examples Analyze Core content mastery
ILO 2 Differentiate between classical conditioning, operant conditioning, and observational learning by identifying defining characteristics in case scenarios Analyze Comparative understanding
ILO 3 Apply operant conditioning principles (reinforcement, punishment, schedules) to design a behavior modification plan for a real-world scenario Apply Practical application
ILO 4 Evaluate the strengths and limitations of learning theories in explaining human behavior across diverse contexts and cultures Evaluate Critical thinking
ILO 5 Collaboratively construct a group presentation that synthesizes learning theories to explain a complex human behavior phenomenon Create Social Constructivism alignment
ILO 6 AI Literacy Demonstrate AI literacy by critically evaluating AI-generated explanations of learning theories, identifying inaccuracies, biases, and limitations, and making independent judgments about when to trust or override AI-generated content Evaluate AI literacy outcome
ILO 7 AI Ethics Reflect on the ethical considerations of using AI to support learning, including maintaining academic integrity and independent reasoning when using AI tools in psychology coursework Evaluate AI literacy + ethical judgment
📐ILO Design Notes — Social Constructivism Alignment
  • ILO #5 emphasises collaboration as an explicit outcome, reflecting Vygotsky's Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD) — students co-construct knowledge within groups.
  • ILOs #1–4 are individually scaffolded to prepare students for the collaborative ZPD activity in ILO #5.
  • ILO #6 addresses AI literacy: using AI critically, evaluating accuracy/bias/limitations, and exercising independent judgment on whether to act on AI content.
  • ILO #7 ensures students develop metacognitive awareness of when and how to use AI responsibly in academic work.
