About this session

      

        In this session you will closely read a short primary source — a document written in
        or near 19th-century Hong Kong — and think through it together with your tutor. There is
        no rush and no trick: every question is meant to help you notice something in the source
        yourself.
      

      

        

          
How this session works

          
    
            
  • Your tutor asks one guiding question at a time and waits for your answer.
    • 
            
  • If you are stuck, ask for a hint — you will always get a nudge before any answer.
    • 
            
  • The three learning objectives and your progress appear in the panel beside the chat.
    • 
          

        

        

          
How to read a primary source

          
    
            
  • Read the whole source once before you answer.
    • 
            
  • Quote the exact words that support your idea.
    • 
            
  • Re-read any detail that seems unusual or vivid.
    • 
            
  • Be ready to revise your first answer as you notice more.
