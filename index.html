Course Overview

This lesson explores teacher professionalism from both local (Hong Kong/Singapore) and international (Finland/USA) perspectives. Through collaborative activities grounded in Social Constructivism, you will analyze, compare, and construct shared understanding of how context shapes professional expectations.

Lesson Structure
