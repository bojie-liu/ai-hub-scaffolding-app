Scaffolding Reading Comprehension through Think-Aloud Modelling and Guided Group Annotation

Course: BEd (English Language) — Teaching Reading in English

Duration: 120 minutes

Class Size: 90 students

Pedagogy: Vygotsky's Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD)

Your Progress: 0% Complete

Lesson Overview

This 120-minute session applies Vygotsky's Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD) to teach reading comprehension scaffolding strategies. You will learn how to make expert reading strategies visible through think-aloud modelling and collaborative annotation.

What You Will Learn

  • Apply think-aloud protocols to make reading strategies explicit for L2 learners
  • Use collaborative annotation codes and discussion prompts to co-construct meaning
  • Analyze how teacher and peer scaffolding moves learners through the ZPD
  • Evaluate scaffolding tools for diverse secondary English learners
  • Design mini-lesson segments integrating think-aloud and group annotation

Pedagogical Framework

"What a child can do with assistance today, she will be able to do by herself tomorrow." — Lev Vygotsky

The lesson follows a gradual-release structure: I do → We do → You do, aligning with ZPD principles of scaffolded instruction.
