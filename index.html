Course Overview

A 150-minute seminar using Social Constructivism to investigate teacher professionalism as a contested, context-dependent concept.

Intended Learning Outcomes (ILOs)

  • Define teacher professionalism and explain why it is a contested concept
  • Compare teacher professionalism frameworks across at least two national contexts
  • Analyse ethical dilemmas in teaching using professional standards
  • Evaluate how socio-cultural factors shape professional identity
  • Reflect critically on your own developing professional identity

Core Concepts

  • Professionalism — Values, behaviours and attitudes that define occupational practice
  • Professional identity — How teachers perceive themselves as professionals
  • Autonomy — Degree of independent judgment teachers exercise
  • Accountability — Mechanisms by which teachers answer to stakeholders
  • CPD — Continuous Professional Development: ongoing learning to improve practice
  • PCK — Shulman's Pedagogical Content Knowledge: subject + teaching knowledge

Expert Group Briefs

🇫🇮 Group A — Finland

Segment 3: Ethical Dilemmas

30 minutes · Apply professional standards to real-world teaching dilemmas.

⏱ 1:20 – 1:50
⚖️

Dilemma Cases

📌 Case A: The Scripted Lesson ▼

A principal requires all teachers to follow a scripted lesson plan word-for-word. A teacher believes her class needs a different approach. What should she do?

Discussion: What tensions exist between institutional compliance and professional autonomy?

📌 Case B: The Parent Demand ▼

A parent demands a grade change, threatening to escalate to the school board. The grade accurately reflects performance. How should the teacher respond?

Discussion: How do professional ethics protect teachers and students in this situation?

📌 Case C: Social Media Post ▼

A trainee teacher posts frustration about a difficult class without naming the school. A colleague recognises the school and reports it. What are the professional implications?

Discussion: Where does the boundary of professional conduct extend beyond the classroom?
