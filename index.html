🧠Pedagogical Framework
Social Constructivism (Vygotsky)

Knowledge is co-constructed through peer interaction, discussion, and collaborative meaning-making. Students work within their Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD), supported by peers and instructor scaffolding.

AI Literacy Integration

AI tools are positioned as stimuli for critical discourse — not authoritative sources. Students evaluate, critique, and override AI outputs, developing independent judgment and ethical awareness.

Bloom's Taxonomy Alignment

ILOs span Analyze → Apply → Evaluate → Create. Activities scaffold progressively from individual comprehension to collaborative synthesis.
