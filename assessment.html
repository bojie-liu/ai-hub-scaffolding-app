📊 Formative Assessment (During Class)

These ongoing checks help track your learning throughout the lesson.

Assessment Purpose Pedagogy Link

Exit ticket (post-test) Show what you learned today Summative snapshot of scaffolded lesson closure

✅ Summative Assessment

End-of-unit quiz (after additional lessons on place value)

Format

  • 10 questions total
  • Multiple choice (place value identification)
  • Short answer (expanded form)
  • Ordering numbers
  • Two word problems involving bundling contexts

Grading Rubric

Level Description
Exceeding (4) Consistently explains place value reasoning using accurate mathematical language; independently solves multi-step application problems.
Meeting (3) Correctly identifies, represents, and compares 3-digit numbers; applies place value in routine word problems.
Developing (2) Identifies place value with teacher prompts; errors occur when comparing numbers involving zeros or renaming.
Beginning (1) Requires significant scaffolding to identify place value; cannot yet consistently represent or compare 3-digit numbers.

🎯 Success Targets

  • 80% of students correctly answer exit ticket Q1 (identifying place value)
  • 70% of students justify comparison on exit ticket Q2 using place-value language
  • Most students transition smoothly from concrete to pictorial representation
  • Student talk includes sentence frames and mathematical vocabulary

💪 Self-Assessment

Rate your confidence using "Fist to Five":

Fist (0): I'm very confused and need more help

1-2 Fingers: I understand a little but need more practice

3 Fingers: I understand most of it

4-5 Fingers: I understand it well and can explain it to others!

Questions to ask yourself:
• Can I build numbers with base-10 blocks?
• Can I compare numbers by looking at place value?
• Can I explain my thinking using math words?
