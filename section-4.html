Summative Assessment: AI Critique & Revision

AI Critique & Revision Assignment

15% of module grade

Use AI as a critical thinking tool — not a shortcut. Generate content with AI, identify its flaws, correct them, and reflect on the process.

Task Steps

  1. Generate: Use an AI tool to produce a 500-word explanation of one learning theory (Behaviourism, Cognitivism, or Social Constructivism). Paste the AI output into your document.
  2. Critically evaluate: Identify at least two inaccuracies, omissions, or biases in the AI output. Support each with evidence from the assigned readings.
  3. Revise: Write a corrected version in 300–400 words that addresses every error or gap identified.
  4. Reflect: Write a 200-word reflection addressing: what changed and why; where human judgment was essential; under what future conditions you would or would not use AI for this kind of task.

AI-Supported Feedback

Turnitin Draft Coach Workflow

Students submit drafts through Turnitin Draft Coach (or a similar AI-feedback tool) before the instructor review stage.

  1. Initial AI feedback: Draft Coach reviews grammar, clarity, and similarity. It flags passive voice, long sentences, and potential plagiarism matches.
  2. Instructor oversight: The instructor reviews AI-generated comments before they reach students, adds pedagogical content feedback, and contextualises AI suggestions.
Example note from instructor: "Passive voice is acceptable and sometimes preferable when describing theoretical positions (e.g., 'learning is said to be…'). Override the AI suggestion if context warrants."

Student Reflection on Feedback

  1. Did you agree with the AI feedback? What might the AI have missed about your argument or context?
  2. Did you override any AI suggestions? Explain the reasoning behind your decision.

🗓️ Calibration Session Teacher Only

Before returning graded work, run a brief class calibration:

  • Share 2–3 anonymised examples of AI feedback from Draft Coach.
  • Class discusses: When should you trust the AI feedback? When should you push back?
  • Record consensus norms on the board for future reference.
AI Integration Principle

This ensures AI serves as a supplement, not a replacement, for human pedagogical judgment.
